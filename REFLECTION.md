# Reflection: IP Address Tracker (React)

I started by creating each file and filling it with fake data. Then I built one feature at a time, top down, so I could get a better understanding of the build process instead of just getting it to work. I used Claude as a tutor to ask questions and to help me keep the project structured, while I typed and tested the code myself.

Overall, the hardest part was just trying to understand React. I needed help with the syntax, and terminology has always been the most difficult part for me. My CSS was acceptable, but it wasn't up to my standards, especially once I looked at the site on my actual phone.

To get through it, I did a lot of reading documentation and asked more questions than I can count. I'd like to say I learned a lot, but cramming my brain with all of this was extremely exhausting. I know I'll need to keep studying the documentation and my notes for it to really stick.

If I had more time, I'd add a way to clear the search history. On mobile, the search bar doesn't remember or offer autofill for previous searches, though that could just be my phone. I'd also fix the Netlify logo covering up the recent searches on the right side of the screen.
